Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 1, 2026

At Generous Corp, we value privacy by design. Solar Energy Powerflow Monitor is built to operate without collecting, storing, or tracking personal data.

1. No Data Collection

We do not collect names, email addresses, or usage behavior. There is no analytics, crash reporting, or third-party ad tracking.

2. App Functionality

To operate, the App may connect to devices and services on your local network in order to:

  • Access your MonitorMySolar dongle to retrieve configuration details used for setting up your MQTT broker (via the dongle details endpoint).
  • Connect to your MQTT broker to read real-time solar inverter data from your MonitorMySolar Universal Solar Inverter Dongle and send charging control commands to your inverter.
  • Communicate with your EVCC server (optional). The App may check whether an EVCC server is reachable on your local network in order to support EV charging management via EVCC.
  • Connect to your InfluxDB server (optional). If configured, the App may connect to your InfluxDB instance to retrieve historical solar and charging data for display in charts and analytics.
  • Connect to a backend server (optional). If configured, the App may communicate with a local backend server for advanced AC charging control features, including SOC-based charge termination and remote push notifications.

All connections are user-configured and connect directly to your own servers and devices. We do not operate or have access to these servers.

3. Permissions

The App requires the following permissions:

  • Network Access: Required to connect to your MQTT broker, EVCC server, InfluxDB server, and backend server.
  • Notifications: Optional. If enabled, the App can display push notifications for charging alerts and Live Activities showing real-time charging progress on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

The App does not request access to Location, Contacts, Files, Photos, Microphone, or Camera.

4. Local Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Connection profiles: MQTT host, port, username, dongle identifier, EVCC server URL, InfluxDB credentials, and backend server configuration
  • Entity mappings: Custom MQTT topic and field mappings for data entities
  • Passwords: MQTT credentials and InfluxDB tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain
  • Preferences: Theme settings, notification preferences, and display options stored in UserDefaults
  • Historical data: Power flow data, charging sessions, and analytics stored locally using SwiftData
  • Device token: If notifications are enabled, an Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token is generated by iOS and sent to your configured backend server (if any) to enable push notifications
  • iCloud sync: If iCloud is enabled on your device, the App will automatically sync and back up your settings using iCloud Key-Value Store.

This data remains on your device (and iCloud account if enabled) and is not transmitted to Generous Corp. If you configure a backend server, the device token and charging status information may be sent to your own server for notification delivery.
