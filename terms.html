1. Service Description

Solar Energy Powerflow Monitor allows users to:

  • View real-time power flow data from their solar inverter system
  • Visualize energy distribution between solar panels, battery, grid, and home consumption
  • Control AC charging (grid-to-battery) with time-based and SOC-based scheduling
  • Access historical solar and charging data via InfluxDB integration (optional)
  • Manage EV charging via EVCC integration (optional)
  • Receive push notifications for charging status and alerts (optional)
  • Monitor charging progress via Live Activities on Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

This app requires the Universal Solar Inverter Dongle from MonitorMySolar to function. The app is provided for personal, non-commercial use only.
